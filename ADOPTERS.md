# Who uses Modulith Rules

Teams that run Modulith Rules in their builds, listed with their permission. A short
line about where it runs helps the next reader decide whether it fits their codebase,
and it helps the project show that the rules hold up outside the test suite.

To add your team, open an issue with the
[adopter template](https://github.com/modular-monolith/modular-monolith-rules/issues/new?template=adopter.yml)
or send a pull request against this file. Company name, a link, and one sentence are
enough. Individual projects and open-source codebases are welcome too.
